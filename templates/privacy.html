Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use Spendly:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense Data: Any expenses you log, including category, amount, date, and optional description.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited, features used, and time spent.

We do not collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers, credit card details, or login credentials for external financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Process and display your expense data as you request
  • Generate spending summaries and visualizations for your personal use
  • Communicate with you about your account, updates, or policy changes
  • Analyze usage trends to improve user experience (aggregated and anonymized)

We do not use your personal expense data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than providing the Service to you.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server hosting Spendly. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Password hashing using industry-standard algorithms (werkzeug.security)
  • Session-based authentication with secure cookies
  • SQL parameterization to prevent injection attacks

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not currently integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share your personal data with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • With your explicit consent

If we add third-party integrations in the future (e.g., for data export or backup), we will update this policy and notify you before any data is shared.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information and expense data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data from our active databases within 30 days, though residual copies may remain in backups for up to 90 days.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability (receive your data in a structured format)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

7. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@spendly.example.com

You can also review our Terms and Conditions.
